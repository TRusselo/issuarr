History and credits

Issuarr continues from Mylar. evilhero created Mylar and wrote most of it. It was rewritten for Python 3 as mylar3/mylar3 and carried on by the community as MylarComics/mylar3.

Issuarr is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Mylar3 or MylarComics teams, so please bring Issuarr questions to Issuarr's issue tracker rather than to them.
